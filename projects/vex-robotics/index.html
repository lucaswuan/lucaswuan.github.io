01 / CONTEXT

Overview

I designed and built a VEX V5 competition robot and programmed its autonomous routines. My work covered the drivetrain, disc intake, and flywheel launcher, with repeated testing and mechanical adjustments between iterations.

02 / CONTRIBUTION

The work

Mechanical design

I designed and built the drivetrain, intake, and flywheel launcher. These systems needed to work together to move around the field, collect discs, and launch them consistently.

Testing & iteration

I iteratively tuned flywheel speed and intake spacing to improve shooting consistency and reduce jams. This work connected observations on the field with changes to the mechanism and its settings.

Autonomous routines

Alongside the mechanical work, I programmed the robot’s autonomous routines. The project combined robot construction, programming, and competition testing.

03 / REFLECTION

Outcome

Our team placed second in Alberta Provincial Robot Skills and qualified for the VEX Robotics World Championship. We competed in Dallas in 2023.
