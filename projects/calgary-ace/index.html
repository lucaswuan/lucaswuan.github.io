01 / CONTEXT

Overview

I co-founded Calgary Ace Learning Center, a student-run nonprofit. My work focuses on tutoring, organizing, and fundraising: contributing time to help students learn while supporting a cause beyond the classroom.

02 / CONTRIBUTION

The work

Teaching & volunteering

I have contributed more than 400 volunteer hours through tutoring and organizing. Teaching is a central part of my involvement, alongside the work needed to help a student-run organization operate.

Fundraising for children’s healthcare

I led fundraising that raised more than $5,000 for the Alberta Children’s Hospital Foundation. This work connected our student-led organization with a broader community purpose.

Building the organization

As a co-founder, I contribute to both the educational work and the organization behind it. I also built its WordPress website to support its online presence.

03 / REFLECTION

Outcome

An ongoing commitment to education and community service, with more than 400 volunteer hours contributed and $5,000+ raised for the Alberta Children’s Hospital Foundation.
