01 / CONTEXT

Overview

I co-founded Calgary Ace Learning Center (CALC), a nonprofit where IB and AP students tutor younger students. All of the proceeds from tutoring go to the Alberta Children’s Hospital Foundation.

I tutor, help run the organization, led our fundraising, and built our website.

02 / CONTRIBUTION

The work

Teaching & volunteering

I have contributed more than 400 volunteer hours to tutoring and organizing. Teaching is the core of what we do: students who have been through demanding courses help younger students build the same skills.

Giving it all to children’s healthcare

Every dollar from tutoring goes to the Alberta Children’s Hospital Foundation. On December 4, 2025, we presented the foundation with a cheque for $5,335, and we signed our names on a heart that is still on the wall there.

Building the website

I built CALC’s website in WordPress. It introduces our services and our team, shares updates on a bulletin page, and lets families book tutoring sessions.

03 / REFLECTION

Outcome

An ongoing commitment to education and community service: more than 400 volunteer hours, and $5,335 donated to the Alberta Children’s Hospital Foundation so far.
